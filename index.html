Buying power over time

In 2005 dollars. The dashed line is $10,000: above it, your money buys more than at the start.

How it works

Buying power. We take the value of your money at the end and remove the price rise since the start. What is left is what it can buy, in start-year dollars.

The coins. Each coin is one dollar. The blue crystal is the share of buying power that was lost. A whole coin kept up with prices or did better.

The data. Yearly total returns from Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern). Inflation is US CPI, December to December. No fees or taxes.
